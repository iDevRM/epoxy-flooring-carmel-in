What Happens When You Call?

A lot of people hesitate to call because they’re not sure if their floor qualifies or worry about getting a hard sales pitch. Here’s exactly what to expect:

  1. We ask a few quick questions about your floor—size, how old the concrete is, any cracks or prior coatings, and what you want to use the space for.
  2. We schedule a free on-site visit at your Carmel home. We bring a moisture meter and take a look at the actual slab—not just photos.
  3. We give you a written quote on the spot for the system that makes sense for your floor. No obligation, no follow-up pressure.
  4. If you want to move forward, we book a date. Most Carmel jobs are on the schedule within 1–2 weeks.
